## 1. Cross-cutting issues affecting every folder

These show up in most photos and have to be dealt with at the editing/curation stage:

1. **Construction debris** — broken concrete, gravel piles, paint buckets, leftover formwork sit in front of facades. Has to be cropped out or, ideally, retaken when the site is clean.
2. **Cars in frame** — branded SUVs (Toyota, Mercedes, Honda) parked directly in front of buildings. Looks like real estate listings rather than a portfolio. Either crop, clone-stamp, or reshoot.
3. **Sky power lines and razor wire** — visible above several buildings (especially Millennium). Ideally clean these out in post.
4. **Building markers** — small "3a", "3c", number plates on walls work for inspections but read amateur. Crop or hide.
5. **Phone screenshots** — the 5 `home building interior` JPGs all show the muted-speaker icon from a video player overlay. They're 150 KB versus the 1–3 MB HEICs. Sharper alternatives almost certainly exist on the original phone — please check.
6. **No professional photography** — no twilight/dusk shots, no drone/aerial, no lifestyle (people, furniture, golden-hour light). Every premium real estate site leans on these.
